# Metro Station Animation

An animated 3D metro station scene built in Blender. A train pulls
into the platform and the doors open, shown through a main camera
and a 360 camera.

## What is in the scene
- Station platform with tiles, safety line, benches, pillars, and a ceiling
- Metro train with bogies, doors, windows, interior seats, and LED displays
- Tunnel with rails, ballast, and sleepers
- Signage including "CENTRAL STATION" and "PLATFORM 1", plus advertisement panels
- Around 30 custom materials and 16 light sources
- Keyframe animation for the train, 12 door leaves, and the camera

## Preview
Full animation: [renders/metro_station_animation.mp4](renders/metro_station_animation.mp4)

## Details
- Software: Blender 5.1
- Animation length: 480 frames
- Render engine: [Cycles or EEVEE]

## How to open
Download MetroStation.blend, open it in Blender, and press the
spacebar to play the animation. Press F12 to render a frame.
